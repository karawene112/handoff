How it works

From one sentence to done, without chasing anyone

You set the rules once. Delegiq does the reminding, the follow-ups and the reporting.

Add your team

Give each person a level and a team. Delegiq creates their login and a temporary password. Team leaders manage only the people below them.

Describe the task

Type one sentence. AI writes the title, steps, deadline and priority, and suggests the person with the right skills and the most room.

Get told when it slips

Reminders go out before the deadline. Late or blocked work emails the person and their boss straight away. Managers get a summary every morning.

AI task writer

Write tasks in seconds

“Adam should prepare the Q4 sales report for the board by tomorrow, it’s urgent.” That’s all you type.

  • Clear title, details and step-by-step checklist
  • Deadline and priority worked out from your words
  • Picks who should do it, based on skills and workload, and says why

Questions

Good to know

Do I need a card for the free trial?

No. You get 14 days with up to 30 people. When the trial ends you can still see and update your work. You just can’t add new tasks or people until you choose a plan.

Can I change plans or cancel?

Yes, anytime, from Settings → Plan & billing → Manage subscription. If you cancel, your plan keeps working until the end of the month you paid for.

Who can see what?

Team members see only their own tasks. Team leaders see the people below them in their own team. The manager sees everything. These rules are enforced by the database itself, not just hidden on the screen.

Where is my data kept?

In a secured database in Frankfurt, in the EU. Each company’s data is kept apart, files are only reachable through links that expire after an hour, and we never sell your data. See the privacy policy.

Does the AI see my company’s data?

Only what the person using it is already allowed to see, and only when they press an AI button. It writes a task draft or a daily brief and nothing is used to train AI models.

How do I pay?

By card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or PayPal. Payments are handled by our reseller, Lemon Squeezy, who also handles sales tax and invoices.

Do you offer refunds?

If something isn’t right in your first 14 days after paying, ask and we’ll refund that payment. See the refund policy.
