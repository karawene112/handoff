Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy explains what information Delegiq collects, why, and what you can do about it. Delegiq is run by Karawene Ben Cheikh, based in Doha, Qatar. For data that companies put into Delegiq about their team, the company is in charge of that data and we process it on its behalf.

What we collect

  • Account details: name, email address, password (stored only as a secure hash) and two-step sign-in settings.
  • Work content: tasks, steps, comments, files, levels, rights, teams and job details that your company adds.
  • Security records: sign-in times, device type, and whether two-step sign-in was used.
  • Billing: your plan and subscription status. Card details go straight to our reseller, Lemon Squeezy. We never see or store them.

How we use it

  • To run Delegiq: show the right tasks to the right people, send the emails you expect, and keep accounts secure.
  • To bill companies on paid plans.
  • To answer support requests and tell managers about important changes.

We do not sell personal data, show ads, or use your content to train AI models.

Who helps us run Delegiq

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and file storage, hosted in Frankfurt, Germany (EU).
  • Resend: sends Delegiq’s emails.
  • Anthropic: runs the AI features. Only the text needed for a request you make is sent, and it is not used to train models.
  • Lemon Squeezy: payments, invoices and sales tax, as merchant of record.
  • GitHub Pages: serves the website files.

These providers only get what they need to do their job, and some of them process data outside your country, under their own security and privacy commitments.

How long we keep it

We keep a company’s data while it uses Delegiq. When the manager deletes the company, its data is deleted. If a plan ends and is not renewed, we may delete the company’s data after 90 days. Billing records are kept as long as tax law requires.

Security

Access rules in the database keep each company’s data apart and limit each person to what their rights allow. Managers must use two-step sign-in, sessions end after inactivity, and file links expire after an hour.

Your choices and rights

You can see and change your details in Delegiq, and your manager can correct or remove team information. You can ask us for a copy of your personal data, or for it to be corrected or deleted. Depending on where you live, you may have more rights under local law, such as Qatar’s Personal Data Privacy Protection Law or the EU GDPR, including complaining to a data protection authority.

Cookies

Delegiq uses your browser’s storage only to keep you signed in and remember simple preferences. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell managers by email before it takes effect.
