Refund Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Every company starts with a free 14-day trial, so you can try Delegiq fully before paying.

Your first payment

If Delegiq isn’t right for you, ask for a refund within 14 days of your first payment and we will refund it in full.

Later payments

Monthly payments after the first are not refunded, but you can cancel at any time and you won’t be charged again. Your plan keeps working until the end of the month you paid for.

Problems on our side

If Delegiq was unavailable for a long time or a feature you paid for did not work, contact us and we will put it right, which can include a full or partial refund.

How refunds are paid

Payments are handled by our reseller, Lemon Squeezy. Refunds go back to the card or account you paid with, usually within 5–10 business days.
